About

Over 12 years I've tested apps used by millions, from The New York Times to an FDA-regulated ultrasound app at Butterfly Network. I work in Playwright, Cypress, Selenium, Appium, Espresso, Jetpack Compose and XCUITest. I have extensive experience running and troubleshooting test suites in CI/CD pipelines on Jenkins, Buildkite, CircleCI, AWS and Firebase.

I use AI tools every day to test faster: Claude, OpenAI Codex and GitHub Copilot for writing and debugging tests, Glean and Gemini for test planning and root-cause analysis, and custom Claude agents I build myself.

I learn fast, like solving problems, and enjoy breaking things on purpose so they don't break for users.
